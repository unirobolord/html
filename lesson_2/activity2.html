🧪 The Formatter's Lab

Welcome to the Formatter's Lab! Here, we explore the fascinating world of formatting and how it can enhance the presentation of information.

🔬 Experiment 1: Bold vs. Strong

The bold tag is used for stylistic purposes, while the strong tag indicates that the text is of strong importance

The Cheetah is the fastest land animal.

You should always read the instructions carefully.

🧪 Experiment 2: Italic vs. Emphasis

The italic tag is used for stylistic purposes, while the emphasis tag indicates that the text is of strong importance.

The Kingdom of Heaven is a film directed by Mel Gibson.

You should always wear a safety goggleswhile operating heavy machinery.

🧪 Experiment 3: Highlight vs. Small

The highlight tag is used to highlight text, while the small tag indicates that the text is of small importance.

Remember : Water boils at 100°C

Note : Always check the expiration date

🧪 Experiment 4: Deleted Text

The deleted tag is used to indicate that text has been deleted.

The old price is no longer valid.

🧪 Experiment 5: Superscript and Subscript

The superscript tag is used to indicate that text should be displayed as superscript, while the subscript tag indicates that text should be displayed as subscript.

The chemical formula for water is H2O.

The mathematics formula for the area of a circle is A = πr2.
